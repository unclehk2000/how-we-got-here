BENIHIME · RTH GAPS (new)
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
• Maps RTH gaps the way OpenGaps maps NWOGs. RTH gap = the overnight move from the prior 16:14 close (the RTH close, 16:15 ET) to the 09:30 ET open. Every gap runs right in price.
• Keep the last 5–60 sessions (default 20).
• The gaps are read from 15-minute bars of the chart's own symbol (the 16:00 bar's close, the 09:30 bar's open), so they are exact on ETH and RTH charts alike. The first build read daily bars, but TradingView's daily futures bars ignore the RTH session, and that measured the 17:00 → 18:00 gap instead; that is fixed.
• Tiers:
  – staircase, the last 5 sessions (rolling): octants plus ±1 SD (dashed) and ±2 SD (solid), gap-size multiples above the top and below the bottom, shaded. Each ±2 SD line carries its gap's weekday (e.g. "Tue +2 SD", "Wed CPI −2 SD"). Release days keep their staircase while they stay on the chart;
  – up to N sessions old (default 10): quarters;
  – older: edges + CE.
  Filled gaps fade. Labels show the date, the release tag and "CE" / "filled"; labels whose CEs sit close together share one.
• Release regime:
  – CPI and NFP (major, orange);
  – PPI, GDP, PCE and retail sales (other, purple);
  – your own dates.
  Actual release dates from the BLS, BEA and Census archives and ALFRED. CPI, NFP and PPI are loaded to Dec 2027; GDP, PCE and retail sales to Dec 2026.
• Card (average RTH gap tracker):
  – live in RTH: today's gap in points and %, and against the 20-session average; the average over N sessions, with how many are loaded; CE and fill with times, or the live odds by 16:14; ±1 SD odds; the odds over the staircase life (5 sessions); the nearest open gap above and below price;
  – outside RTH: the 16:14 close, "if it opened now", the coming session's releases, and the last gap's result.
• Research, NQ + ES + RTY 1-minute 2018–26, 6,169 gaps:
  – in RTH, 78% reach the CE and 62% fill; within 5 sessions 91% / 84%; within 20 sessions 96% / 92%;
  – size against the 20-session average decides it. RTH fill rate: under 0.5× 88%, 0.5–1× 64%, 1–1.5× 49%, 1.5–2.5× 38%, 2.5×+ 22%. Steady in both periods and on each symbol;
  – CPI and NFP gaps run 1.4–1.5× the size of normal-day gaps (p < 0.001);
  – PPI, GDP, PCE and retail sales move price at 08:30 (1.8–3.6× a normal 08:30–08:34 range), but their gaps were not reliably bigger;
  – for its size, no release-day gap behaved differently;
  – a gap still unfilled after 5 sessions filled within the next 15 sessions 49% of the time.

BAKUDŌ 58 · MACRO LINK (new)
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
• A card that shows which macro force the index is moving with, and how hard: the dollar (DXY) and oil (CL).
• Three regimes, worked out from yesterday's closes:
  – WEAK $: DXY below its 200-day average. The index follows oil; 1% CL ≈ +0.16–0.22%.
  – STRONG $: DXY above its 200-day average. The dollar leads; 1% DXY ≈ −0.75 to −0.95%. Oil barely matters.
  – OIL SHOCK: CL's front month is 15%+ a year above the second month, and CL is up 10%+ in 20 days. Both turn against the index: 1% DXY ≈ −1.05 to −1.50%, 1% CL ≈ −0.10 to −0.13%. Together they explain about 30% of the day's move, against 6–13% otherwise.
• Rows:
  – WATCH: what to watch in this regime, and the index points per 1%.
  – DXY and OIL: each one's move today and the index move that goes with it.
  – TODAY: the index's move and how much of it the dollar and oil account for.
  – LIVE β: the last 60 days' sensitivity against the tested values.
  – SHOCK: the distance to the oil-shock rule.
  – CARRY: the index carry (next contract over front, annualised) and how many points a day the front month gives up to cash.
  Mobile shows four rows.
• An alert fires when the regime changes.
• Memory: the daily histories behind LIVE β are handed over on the last bar only, and each request reads just the last ~100 daily bars. Before, a copy was stored for every bar, which ran out of memory on 1-minute charts ("Memory limits exceeded"). LIVE β can be switched off.
• Research: NQ, ES and RTY daily data 2018–26, with DXY 5-minute (2023–26) and CL 1-minute (FOMC weeks). Each regime effect held pooled (p < 0.05, errors clustered by date), in 2018–24 and 2025–26, and on every symbol.
• What did not hold:
  – neither DXY nor CL led the index: not over the next minutes, the rest of the day, the next session or the next week;
  – a gap between the index and what the dollar implies did not close by 16:00;
  – no reliable quarter or month effect in either link;
  – the index carry did not lead the next week's return, range, close in the premium of the prior week, or a take of its high or low.
  So the card is context, not a signal.

SHIKAI / BANKAI SESSION MAP (session levels fix)
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
• Fix: the Asia and London 25% and 50% levels no longer move while the session trades inside its range.
  – They are now retracement levels of the session's high–low, measured from whichever extreme came last. A session that made its high first and its low later is measured up from the low.
  – They change only when the session makes a new high or low.
  – Before, they were a share of the open–close body, so they moved with every tick until the session closed.
  – The old method is still available: "25 / 37.5 / 50% of" → Body (open–close).
• New: a 37.5% level (dashed), on by default, with its own toggle and colour. It applies to the lines on the chart and the ticks on the session candles.
• The card's LDN 25% row uses the same definition and shows the leg (up or down).
  – NQ + ES + RTY 2018–26, after 05:00: London's 25% was reached by 11:00 on 91% of days and by 16:59 on 94%.
  – The 37.5% on 90% / 93%, and the 50% on 86% / 90%.
  – Asia's levels (from 00:00) were reached at about the same rates.
  – Steady in both periods and on every symbol.

BAKUDŌ 58 · FRACTAL MODEL (added, 16 Oct 2026)
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
• The C2 → C3 fractal model now runs inside Bakudō 58, on the chart and on the card. It is written from the model's description; no code was taken from the two shared scripts.
• Pairs:
  – 1m chart with 15-minute candles;
  – 1H chart with daily candles (CME day, 18:00–17:00 ET).
  "Auto" uses 15-minute candles on charts of 5 minutes or less, and daily candles on longer intraday charts. Other chart timeframes still run, but the card marks the odds "untested".
• On the chart:
  – the C2 label and the sweep of C1's low or high. When C2's extreme breaks, the label becomes XC2 and the setup keeps only its label, the sweep and a grey CISD line.
  – C2's midpoint (EQ) and the T-spot (C3's open to EQ) during C3.
  – the CISD line, labelled "CISD · in C2" when it confirmed inside C2. An early CISD shows as a dashed line while the candle is still forming.
  – projections +1, +2, +2.5, +4 and +4.5 with their odds, ticked ✓ when hit.
  – the protected swing (●), greyed if it is taken before C4 ends.
  – a preview of the last 4 higher-timeframe candles right of price (offset 12 bars), tagged C1–C4, with a countdown to the close.
• Card: View → "Card shows" Both / Fractal / Macro.
  – Fractal rows: C2, C3, C2 LOW (or HIGH), CISD, TARGETS, PROTECT.
  – Both shows FRACTAL and MACRO sections.
  – Fractal only gives a neutral header with the pair, since the regime made no difference to these odds.
  – Mobile shows C2, C3 and CISD, plus WATCH (Both) or PROTECT (Fractal).
• Alerts: C2, CISD and protected swing (on "Any alert() function call").
• Memory: no requested arrays. The module keeps at most 10 higher-timeframe candles and the kept setups (default 2). History look-backs are capped at 300 bars. Drawing limits are raised to 500 lines and labels and 100 boxes.
• Research: NQ, ES and RTY 1-minute data 2018–26 (1m–15m: 248k C2s; 1H–1D: 3,089). Each result held pooled (p < 0.05, errors clustered by date), in 2018–24 and 2025–26, and on every symbol.
  Figures are 15m / 1D:
  – Where C2 closes in its own range decides C3. By close-location quarter, C3 took C2's high (bull; mirrored for bear):
      15m: 21 / 36 / 55 / 78%
      1D: 20 / 39 / 58 / 83%
  – Against other candles that closed in the same place, a C2 is not followed by more expansion (−1.6 / −3.2 points). The pattern adds nothing beyond where it closes.
  – C2's swept extreme held through C3 slightly more often than other candles' at the same close location: +1.7 / +3.4 points, and +2.8 / +7.4 when the CISD confirmed inside C2.
  – Once C3 trades back to C2's EQ, the odds that it still takes C2's high fall to 21 / 35 / 47 / 64% (15m).
  – Projections reached before C4 ended:
      +1: 72 / 80%
      +2: 46 / 57%
      +2.5: 36 / 49%
      +4: 20 / 32%
      +4.5: 16 / 28%
  – Protected swings (1m–15m only) held to the end of C4 9.5 points more often than other 1-minute swings with the same bars left and distance. 49% held overall. By bars left in C4 when marked:
      ≤5: 93%
      6–10: 77%
      11–15: 64%
      16–20: 52%
      21+: 39%
    A CISD inside C2 added 4.8 points.
• What did not hold:
  – once close location is accounted for, an early CISD does not raise the odds that C3 takes C2's other extreme;
  – the dollar / oil regime made no difference to any of the fractal odds;
  – protected swings on 1H–1D were not tested, so the card shows "untested".
